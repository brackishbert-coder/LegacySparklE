## Overall Assessment (Neutral)

The roadmap is **internally consistent** and **incrementally extensible**, but it mixes:

* well-defined, low-risk engineering improvements
* speculative mechanisms that introduce significant instability risk
* several features whose value cannot be validated without additional instrumentation or ablation controls

The main risk is **complexity accumulation without falsifiability**. Several expansions depend on signals that are not yet proven to be reliable causal indicators rather than correlated observables.

---

## Item-by-Item Technical Evaluation

### 1. Entropy-Weighted Eviction

**Assessment:** Sound and standard.

This is equivalent to prioritized experience replay with an entropy-based priority function.
Low implementation risk.
High likelihood of measurable effect.

**Primary concern:**
Entropy must be normalized against input amplitude and window size, or it will overweight noise-dominated states.

**Verdict:** Implement early.

---

### 2. Localized Manifold Blooming

**Assessment:** High risk.

Node-splitting based on Same-BMU MSD assumes that:

* MSD reflects intrinsic manifold complexity rather than training instability
* local topology expansion will reduce error instead of amplifying it

Neither assumption is guaranteed.

**Primary concern:**
Without a strict contraction mechanism and a hard cap on total nodes, this can cause irreversible topology inflation.

**Verdict:** Defer until long-term stability metrics exist.

---

### 3. Dynamic Confounder Selection

**Assessment:** Mathematically plausible, operationally fragile.

Rolling selection of confounders introduces non-stationarity into the residualization itself. This can invalidate causal interpretation unless the selection process is itself controlled.

**Primary concern:**
The validator becomes self-referential: changes in residualization alter the very metrics used to select confounders.

**Verdict:** Prototype offline only.

---

### 4. Topological Persistence Mapping

**Assessment:** Necessary but underspecified.

Comparing manifolds across time is required for several later mechanisms. However, “personality drift” needs a formally defined distance metric, not a conceptual one.

**Primary concern:**
If the comparator is not metric-consistent, downstream logic (immune response, freeze decisions) will behave unpredictably.

**Verdict:** Implement before any corrective mechanisms.

---

### 5. Heartbeat-Gated Learning

**Assessment:** Mechanically harmless, potentially useful.

Gating learning updates by a periodic function does not change convergence guarantees if the duty cycle is fixed and learning rates are adjusted accordingly.

**Primary concern:**
If heartbeat phase is allowed to modulate multiple parameters simultaneously (LR, sigma, blooming), coupling effects may cause oscillations.

**Verdict:** Safe if limited to learning rate only.

---

### 6. Coherence-Based Sigma Regulation

**Assessment:** Reasonable feedback controller.

Adjusting neighborhood radius based on path efficiency aligns with SOM theory. However, coherence must be smoothed heavily to avoid reacting to transient noise.

**Primary concern:**
Positive feedback loops if sigma affects coherence faster than coherence stabilizes.

**Verdict:** Implement with hysteresis and rate limits.

---

### 7. Synthetic Stress Testing

**Assessment:** Useful for diagnostics, not learning.

Injecting known perturbations is valid for measuring recovery behavior, but running these tests inline risks contaminating training statistics.

**Primary concern:**
Stress tests must be isolated from normal learning and excluded from metric baselines.

**Verdict:** Tooling-only, offline or scheduled.

---

### 8. Multi-Band BabySOMs

**Assessment:** Sound architectural decomposition.

Parallel feature maps over frequency bands are standard in signal processing and neural models.

**Primary concern:**
The AdultSOM must be explicitly invariant to band dominance, or one band will overpower the others.

**Verdict:** Low risk, good return.

---

### 9. Real-Time Fidelity Alarms

**Assessment:** Necessary safety check.

Detecting synthesis distortion and freezing learning is a straightforward integrity mechanism.

**Primary concern:**
False positives if synthesis artifacts are not well-characterized statistically.

**Verdict:** Implement with conservative thresholds.

---

### 10. Distributed Telemetry

**Assessment:** Pure engineering concern.

Separating visualization and logging from the learning loop improves determinism and latency.

**Primary concern:**
Network jitter should never back-pressure the learning thread.

**Verdict:** Implement whenever performance requires it.

---

### 11. Multi-Step TMR Prediction

**Assessment:** High complexity for uncertain benefit.

Longer-horizon prediction increases sensitivity to rhythmic structure but also multiplies error sources.

**Primary concern:**
Prediction accuracy may degrade to the point that TMR no longer reflects meaningful surprise.

**Verdict:** Defer until single-step TMR is quantitatively validated.

---

### 12. Automated Hyper-Parameter Gardening

**Assessment:** Risky without strong ground truth.

Slow adaptation is preferable to manual tuning, but the controller needs clearly defined target regimes.

**Primary concern:**
The system may optimize toward a stable but uninformative state (e.g., high smoothness, low diversity).

**Verdict:** Implement only after ablation studies.

---

### 13. Localized Immune Reset

**Assessment:** Highest risk component.

Local reinitialization can fix isolated failures, but it can also destroy legitimately learned structure if the detector is imperfect.

**Primary concern:**
False positives cause irreversible information loss.

**Verdict:** Last to implement; requires extensive logging and replay.

---

## Key Structural Risks (Summary)

1. **Signal validity risk**
   Several mechanisms assume metrics are causal indicators rather than correlates.

2. **Feedback coupling**
   Multiple adaptive loops acting on overlapping parameters increase instability risk.

3. **Irreversibility**
   Blooming and immune resets are destructive operations without rollback.

4. **Verification gap**
   Many features lack clear success/failure criteria.
